Privacy

Wheels Off · last updated 2026-10-16

Wheels Off is a browser game made and run by tuxnotfound (github.com/tuxnotfound). Write to tuxnotfound@cioga.eu with any question about this page or your data.

There are no accounts, no cookies and no ad trackers. The game keeps a little in your browser so it remembers your records, and a little on its server so the leaderboards work and I can tell whether people play.

In your browser

Kept in your browser's local storage and sent nowhere: your personal records, this week's best time trial run and its ghost, the last name you typed, whether the music and the ghost are on, and the last copy of the leaderboards, so the game works offline. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes all of it.

On the server

The game's database runs on Cloudflare D1, in western Europe. It keeps:

  • Runs you sign onto a leaderboard: the name you type, the score or time, the week, and for a time trial the path your rider took (the ghost other players race).
  • Runs you share as a challenge: the time, the path, and your name if you have one.
  • Play counts: for each visit where you ride, a random id made for that visit only (it is not stored in your browser), where the visit came from (a tag in the link, such as ?ref=share, or the site that linked here), how many runs you rode and for how many seconds, and the day. I use these to count players and how many come back.
  • A salted hash of your IP address with each of the above: your address mixed with a secret and put through a one-way function. I cannot turn it back into your address, but the same address always gives the same hash. It limits how many runs one address can send, and it is how play counts tell a returning player from a new one.

Visit statistics and hosting

Cloudflare hosts the site and counts page views with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and no local storage. As the host, Cloudflare handles your IP address to deliver each request, under its data processing terms with me. The fonts are served from this site, not from a font service.

The share buttons open X's own post page or copy text to your clipboard. Nothing goes to X unless you post there.

Why, and for how long

I keep this because I have a legitimate interest in running the game, keeping the leaderboards fair, and knowing whether people play it (Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR).

  • Play counts are deleted after 13 months.
  • Signed runs and shared challenges stay for as long as the game runs, since they are the leaderboards and the challenges other players open. I will take yours down if you ask.

Your rights

You can ask what I hold about you, ask me to correct or delete it, or object to how I use it: write to tuxnotfound@cioga.eu. Since I only hold a hash of your address, tell me your name on the boards and roughly when you played so I can find your runs. You can also complain to your data protection authority; in Portugal that is the CNPD (cnpd.pt).
